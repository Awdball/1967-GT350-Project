# 1967 Shelby GT350 Restomod Project

Build documentation, parts cross-referencing, and compatibility tracking for a **1967 Shelby GT350 Restomod** engineered for **Street, Autocross, and Road Course** driving.

---

## Build Philosophy & Goals
- **Dual-Purpose Performance:** Responsive street manners combined with autocross and road-course durability (baffled oil control, high-capacity cooling, modern coilover/3-link geometry, and big brakes).
- **100% Mechanical Fallback (SHTF Resilience):** Critical systems are kept purely mechanical—or easily revertible to a 100% mechanical setup with minimal work.
- **Shade-Tree Serviceability:** Favor widely available, well-documented components that any capable shade-tree mechanic can diagnose and repair without proprietary tools or dealer-only diagnostics.
- **Authentic GT350 Proportions:** Keeping a 302-deck (`8.200"`) small-block Ford (`363ci`) for period-correct weight distribution, fitment, and Cobra/GT350 engine bay aesthetics.

---

## Build Overview by System

### 1. Engine (Acquired — Undressed Longblock)
- **Builder / Model:** FordStrokers (Woody) **363 Liberator** (Dart SHP 4-bolt block, `4.125" × 3.400"`, Liberty forged crank, Scat H-beam rods, Wiseco 2618 forged 14cc dish pistons, **`10.2:1` CR**)
- **Balance:** **Internally Balanced (`0 oz` neutral balance)** — *requires `0 oz` harmonic damper & `0 oz` flywheel/flexplate*
- **Heads & Valvetrain:** AFR 195 Competition `#1381` (`58cc`, angle-plug, `+0.125"` raised exhaust ports, `7/16"` studs), Trick Flow `1.6` roller rockers, Morel `#5323` link-bar hydraulic roller lifters, **COMP Cams `#35-000-9` ("FIEGENER")** billet hydraulic roller cam (`226°/234° @ .050"`, `.563"/.566"` lift, `110°` LSA, `106°` ICL, `1-3-7-2-6-5-4-8` firing order)
- **Induction & Valve Covers:** Edelbrock Performer RPM Air-Gap `#7521` (`4150` square-bore, `4.90"` pad height); Ford **"Cobra" solid block-letter** cast aluminum valve covers *(verify rocker/baffle clearance)*
- **Oiling & Timing Cover:** Moroso `#20535` 7-qt road-race baffled front-sump pan (`8.0"` deep, passenger kickout, pan-mounted dipstick) + Melling `M68` pump; Fox Body timing cover (**no fuel pump eccentric installed**)

> See [`ENGINE_SPEC.md`](./ENGINE_SPEC.md) for the complete spec sheet, machine work, and 10-point dressing checklist.

### 2. Exhaust (Acquired)
- **Headers:** **FPA (Stan — Ford Powertrain Applications)** custom ceramic-coated headers built specifically for `363` + `AFR 195` + manual transmission + `UNI-8010830-01` Unisteer rack + `IDI-1120643051` IDIDIT column.

### 3. Drivetrain (Transmission Planned / Rear Axle Acquired)
- **Transmission (Planned):** **Modern Driveline Tremec TKX** complete auto-to-manual swap kit *(must specify `0 oz` neutral-balance flywheel)*. Currently have a loose 4-speed Toploader (unknown gearset, no bellhousing).
- **Rear Axle Housing & Axles (Acquired):** **Quick Performance 1964–1966 Mustang 9" Package**
  - `'64–'66` width (`2"` narrower than '67) paired with **Mustangs to Fear (MTF) mini-tubs** (moved inward to frame rails) for wider tires / deeper wheel lip
  - Fabricated Big Web housing, `1/4"` heavy-wall 3" tubes (ready for 3-link welding), **Billet Big Ford New Style (Torino) `3/8"` ends**
  - QP `31-spline` alloy axles, `5×4.5"` bolt pattern, `1/2"-20 × 3"` studs *(requires open-ended lug nuts)*
- **Third Member (Acquired):** **Eaton Detroit Truetrac `31-spline`, `3.70:1` gear** (`B01M6Z8SYZ`) — 100% mechanical helical-gear LSD (no clutches), **1350 Billet Yoke & U-bolts**.

### 4. Chassis, Suspension, Steering & Brakes (Acquired — Street or Track)
- **Suspension:** `SOT-PACKAGE1` Front & Rear Coilover System (`SOT-3LINK-U-BRACKET-FIXTURE` 3-link rear), `SOT-BBSPINDLE` Billet Big Drop Spindles (+ Timken bearings/seals), `SCD-C7ZZ-5482-B` `1-1/8"` Front Sway Bar, `SOT-EXPORT` Billet Export Brace, `SOT-QRSTB` Quick-Release Monte Carlo Bar
- **Steering:** `UNI-8010830-01` Unisteer Power Rack & Pinion (Late '67–70; requires **1,100 PSI low-flow** PS pump, `17mm DD` pinion), `SOT-DROPTIERODS`, `IDI-1120643051` IDIDIT Retrofit Tilt Column
- **Brakes:**
  - **Front:** `SOT-13X125-2PC-4P-ALUM-LEADING` (`13" × 1.25"` 2-piece rotor, 4-piston forged aluminum calipers — **requires `17"+` wheels**)
  - **Rear:** `ST-12BKR-T` (`12" × .810"` rear disc kit for **Torino** bearing ends)
  - **Master Cylinder:** `WIL-261-15544-BK` Wilwood Compact Tandem `1"` Bore Manual M/C w/ proportioning valve (no vacuum booster needed)

### 5. Fuel System, Body, Interior & Wheels
- **Fuel Tank & Filler (Acquired):** Mustangs to Fear (MTF) side-fill fuel tank (**built-in EFI pump**) + rear sail-panel mounted **Radium Engineering** fuel-filler neck *(filler piping/hose to tank still needed)*
- **Carburetor & Regulator (Shortlisted):** `750 CFM` 4150 Double Pumper (`FR-76750HB`, `FR-4779CE`/`C`, `FRBR-67213`) *(+ optional Carb-Cheater)*; **Aeromotive `13301`** bypass regulator (`3–20` & `20–65 PSI` springs — covers 4150, Weber 48 IDF, & Borla 8-stack EFI)
- **Ignition (Shortlisted):** **MSD `8479`** small-cap Pro-Billet distributor (**melonized/steel gear** for `#35-000-9` billet cam; clears Weber 48 IDFs) + **MSD `6AL` (`6425`)** + Blaster SS/HVC coil *(reusable across all 3 induction paths)*
- **Safety & Interior (Acquired):** Mustangs to Fear Shelby-style roll bar; refurbished factory seats
- **Body & Chassis Rigidity:** Painted **Ford Antimatter Blue**; **Mustangs to Fear (MTF) mini-tubs** (moved to frame rails) + **convertible inner rockers & seat pan** retrofitted for added chassis rigidity
- **Wheels (Planned):** Custom 3-piece **Augment Wheel Company** (`17"+`, `5×4.5"`, open-ended lugs; pending remote car measurements & SOT front brake template check)

## Open Gaps & Critical Compatibility Rules
1. **Engine Dressing & Accessory Drive (Shortlisted: Holley Mid-Mount SBF):**
   - To run both a **mechanical fuel pump** and **power steering** on the Holley Mid-Mount, you must install a **fuel pump eccentric**, grind the designated timing cover clearance wall, and add **Holley `97-436` PS Relocation Kit + remote reservoir**.
   - Match your **`0 oz` harmonic damper** with the correct Holley crank spacer (`97-446`/`447`/`448`), and ensure the PS pump is valved to **1,100 PSI low-flow** for the Unisteer rack.
2. **Cooling (Shortlisted — Texas A/C + Road Course):** **DeWitts `24.5"` Pro-Series Manual Radiator** (`32-4138006M` / Black Ice™) + **`32-SP8007` Dual 12" SPAL Fan & Shroud Kit** (`~2,200 CFM`, dual relays, staged for temp + A/C; uses `24"` core-support brackets with zero cutting). Budget alt: **Cold Case `FOM587K`**. *(Holley Mid-Mount cannot run a mechanical clutch fan).*
3. **Ignition & Electrical (Needed):** Billet-cam-compatible distributor gear (melonized/steel per FordStrokers cam spec) + shade-tree-serviceable ignition.
4. **Clearance Checks Before Final Assembly:**
   - Moroso `#20535` front-sump pan (`8.0"` deep) vs. Unisteer rack
   - Cobra valve covers over Trick Flow `1.6` roller rockers (`0.060"–0.080"` min clay check)
   - `4.90"` Edelbrock `#7521` Air-Gap + air cleaner vs. 1967 Shelby hood & `SOT-QRSTB` Monte Carlo bar
